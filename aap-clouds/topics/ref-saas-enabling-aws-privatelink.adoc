= Enabling {AWSPrivateLink} connectivity

[role="_abstract"]
To enable private link connectivity, submit a customer support ticket and the Red{nbsp}Hat team will work with you on the next steps. 

[NOTE]
====
You must create *two* separate support tickets for bi-directional connectivity:  

* One enabling {AWSPrivateLink} connectivity from Customer VPC *into the* control plane
* One enabling {AWSPrivateLink} connectivity *out of the* control plane to Customer VPC. 
====

== DNS Resolution and Custom Domains

When using {AWSPrivateLink}, you will also configure custom domain names for the AAP endpoints. These hostnames must be resolvable by the Red Hat managed service, not just by your infrastructure.

See link:https://docs.redhat.com/en/documentation/ansible_on_clouds/2.x/html/red_hat_ansible_automation_platform_service_on_aws/saas-aws#con-saas-dns-resolution-requirements[DNS Resolution Requirements for Custom Domains] for detailed information on:

* The distinction between customer-side DNS and service-side DNS
* Common DNS misconfigurations
* How to validate that your hostnames are resolvable by the service
* Split-horizon DNS considerations

[IMPORTANT]
====
Do not configure custom hostnames in {PlatformNameShort} until you have verified that they are resolvable by the Red Hat managed service, not just your internal network.
====
