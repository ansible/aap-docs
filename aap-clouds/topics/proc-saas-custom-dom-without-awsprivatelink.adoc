= Setting up a custom domain without {AWSPrivateLink}

[role="_abstract"]
If you are not planning to connect to the {PlatformNameShort} UI or use {AutomationMesh} through {AWSPrivateLink}, complete the following steps to configure your DNS.

.Prerequisites

* Ensure the custom domain hostnames you plan to use are resolvable by the Red Hat managed control plane and service components.
* Verify that your custom domain is properly configured in your DNS provider.
* Ensure the hostnames you configure exist in a DNS zone that is resolvable by the Red Hat service, not just your internal network.

.Procedure

. Identify the canonical names of the load balancers of your deployment. 
+
You can use a DNS lookup on the Red Hat-generated URL to identify the DNS names for both load balancers:
+
----
Shell

# Replace the URL with the "platform" URL of your deployment
dig platform.cus-<id>.aws.ansiblecloud.com
----
+

----
Shell

# Replace the URL with the "mesh-ingress" URL of your deployment 
dig mesh-ingress-0.cus-<id>.aws.ansiblecloud.com
----
+
[IMPORTANT]
====
*DNS Resolution Requirements*

The hostnames you configure must be resolvable by the Red Hat managed service, not just by your infrastructure.

If you create a custom DNS entry that exists only in your customer-private DNS zone (accessible only within your VPC), the service will NOT be able to resolve it.

For example:

* ✓ CORRECT: `platform.example.com` → resolvable via PrivateLink-enabled DNS
*  ✗ WRONG: `platform.example.com` → only in customer private DNS zone, not resolvable by service

The CNAME you create must point to a name that the Red Hat service can resolve.
====

. Create DNS CNAME records for your custom domain using the following hostnames pointing to the DNS names identified in the previous step:
* platform (for example, `platform._exampledomain_.com`) → `cus-xxxxx-alb-11111111.us-east-1.elb.amazonaws.com`
* mesh-ingress-0 (for example, `mesh-ingress-0._exampledomain_.com`)  → `xxxxx.elb.us-east-1.amazonaws.com`
* mesh-ingress-1 (for example, `mesh-ingress-1._exampledomain_.com`)  → `xxxxx.elb.us-east-1.amazonaws.com`

.Validation

After configuring your custom domain DNS records:

. From your network, verify the hostname resolves correctly:
.. Run: `nslookup platform.<yourdomain>.com`
.. Confirm it resolves to your platform's IP address or endpoint

. Confirm that the Red Hat managed service can also resolve your custom hostname:
.. Contact link:{CustomerSupport}[Red Hat support] to validate service-side DNS resolution
.. Provide your custom domain name and the DNS records you configured
.. Red Hat support can verify that the service can reach your configured endpoints

. Do not proceed with {PlatformNameShort} configuration until you have confirmed service-side DNS resolution.
