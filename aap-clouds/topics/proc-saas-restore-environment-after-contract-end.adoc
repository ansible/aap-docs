= Restoring your environment after contract end

[role="_abstract"]
If your AWS subscription was not renewed within the 72-hour window and your environment was shut down, you can still restore it within 14 days.

.Prerequisites

* Your Cluster ID (available from your {SaaSonAWS} console).
* Your new AWS subscription ID (from your AWS Marketplace subscription).

.Procedure

. On the AWS Marketplace, subscribe to {SaaSonAWS} again using the same AWS account.
. Wait for approximately 30 minutes for automatic environment linking to occur.
. Log in to your {SaaSonAWS} environment using your original credentials.
+
If your environment does not automatically reconnect:

.. Open a support ticket with Red Hat Support.
.. Provide your Cluster ID and new AWS subscription ID.
.. Support will link your new subscription to your retained environment data.

.Verification

After automatic linking or support assistance, verify that you can access your automation content and execution history as before.
