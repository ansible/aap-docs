= Subscription scenario outcomes

[role="_abstract"]
The following table describes what happens based on your subscription renewal actions:

*End-of-contract lifecycle scenarios*
[options="header"]
|====
| Scenario | Timeline | Outcome
| AWS subscription renewed or replaced | Within 72 hours of expiration | Environment continues uninterrupted; no data loss
| New AWS subscription established | Between 72 hours and 14 days post-shutdown | Environment data may be restorable; contact support to establish the link
| No new AWS subscription established | After 14 days post-shutdown | Environment is permanently deleted; recovery is not possible
|====
