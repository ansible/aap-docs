= Critical security patching and environment supportability

[role="_abstract"]
Maintain network and cluster configurations that allow automated Red Hat updates so your managed Ansible Automation Platform environment stays patched against critical CVEs and avoids temporary SRE isolation.

Red Hat is updating its operational protocols to ensure all managed Ansible Automation Platform environments align with evolving IT security standards and industry best practices. Due to the increasing frequency and severity of _Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures_ (CVEs), Red Hat must be able to upgrade and patch environments. In managed environments where customer configuration may impact Red Hat’s ability to perform patching, customers must ensure their network and cluster configurations do not block required automated maintenance or update mechanisms.

In the event of a critical CVE where an environment cannot be patched due to customer-controlled configurations, the _Site Reliability Engineering_ (SRE) team reserves the right to temporarily halt or isolate the affected environment until remediation can be completed. This measure will be taken when the security risk of keeping an unpatched deployment online poses a severe threat to the environment or broader infrastructure.
