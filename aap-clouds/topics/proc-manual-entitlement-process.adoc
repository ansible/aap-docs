= Manual Entitlement Process

[role="_abstract"]
If you cannot use either automated option, you can manually entitle your subscription.

.Procedure

. Navigate to the {PlatformNameShort} managed application listing in the Azure Marketplace.
. Locate the *Before you Begin* section in the offer overview.
. Click the link within the text:
+
"Click here to enable your Red Hat product subscription and to access Red Hat support."

. Sign in to the Red Hat Hybrid Cloud Console using your Red Hat account.
. Review the submission confirmation message.
. Check the email inbox associated with your Red Hat account for a verification email.
. Click the verification link in the email to activate the support entitlement.
+
[IMPORTANT]
====
Entitlements associated through the manual method do not renew automatically and require manual renewal upon expiration.
====
