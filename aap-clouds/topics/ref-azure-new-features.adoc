= New features and change

[role="_abstract"]
The following new features and enhancements are available for Managed Application customers in the {AAPonAzureNameShort} general availability release on Nov 3rd, 2026.

[cols="10%,90%", options="header"]
|===
| Date | Feature
| Nov 4th 2026 a| *Resolve Azure Policy Restrictions to Allow Automated AKS Upgrades for {AAPonAzureNameShort}*

*Target Audience*: Administrators of {AAPonAzureName} (Managed Application)

*Overview*

Red Hat manages the infrastructure and operations for your {AAPonAzureNameShort} deployment, including the underlying _Azure Kubernetes Service_ (AKS) clusters. {Azure} has introduced a policy change that enforces automatic platform upgrades for any AKS cluster operating on unsupported Kubernetes versions starting June 1, 2027 (Reference Tracking ID: 6H03-S40).

To maintain service stability, compliance, and supportability, Red Hat must be able to perform these routine version upgrades on your managed clusters.

*Critical Impact: Customer-Enforced Azure Policies*

If your organization has applied subscription-level or tenant-level Azure Policies that restrict AKS version upgrades, lock down network endpoints, or block Red Hat management activities within the managed resource group, your deployment may fail required maintenance updates.

Failure to permit these upgrades before the June 1, 2027 deadline could result in:

* Underlying AKS clusters entering an unsupported state with Microsoft Azure.

* Red Hat being blocked from applying critical security patches, bug fixes, and cluster lifecycle operations.

* Compliance gaps and potential disruption to your automation workloads.

After the June 1, 2027 milestone, Azure enforces a strict 60-day window following Kubernetes end-of-support before platform-initiated upgrades occur automatically.

*Required Customer Actions*

. *Review Azure Policy Assignments*: Check subscription-level Azure Policies or management group rules applied to your AAP on Azure subscription to ensure they do not prevent AKS node image or Kubernetes version upgrades.

. *Follow Red Hat Action Requests*: When Red Hat Support communicates specific policy remediation steps, exemptions, or update windows for your managed deployment, address them promptly to ensure management permissions remain operational.

. *Plan Maintenance Windows*: If you require specific blackout dates or coordinated timing for cluster maintenance, contact Red Hat Support in advance of scheduled upgrade notices.

*Questions & Support*

If you need guidance regarding which Azure Policy assignments may conflict with Red Hat managed operations, or to discuss upcoming upgrade windows for your {PlatformNameShort} environment, submit a ticket through the Red Hat Customer Portal. 
|===
