= Method 2: Deploying the Red Hat Subscription Support Registration Offering (Existing Deployments)

[role="_abstract"]
The Azure Marketplace provides a no-cost listing titled "Red Hat Subscription Support Registration." This connects your Azure account to your Red Hat account, automatically maintaining active support entitlements for as long as your Ansible Automation Platform deployment is active.

.Procedure

. Log in to the link:https://portal.azure.com/[Azure Portal].
. In the search bar, navigate to *Marketplace*.
. Search for *Red Hat Subscription Support Registration*..
. Select the appropriate listing:
.. *Billed outside EMEA*: Select the offering published by *Red Hat Inc*.
.. *Billed inside EMEA*: Select the offering published by *Red Hat Limited*.
.. Verify that the listing indicates *Starts at 'free'*.
. Click *Subscribe*.
. Select an existing *Resource Group* or create a new one.
. Enter a name for the subscription.
. Verify that *Recurring billing* is set to *On* (there is no charge).
. Click btn:[Review + subscribe], then click btn:[Subscribe].
. When provisioned, select *Configure account now*. You will be redirected to the Red Hat Hybrid Cloud Console.
. Sign in with your Red Hat account if prompted.
. Select *I have read and agree to the terms and conditions*, then click btn:[Connect accounts].
. Wait for the confirmation message indicating whether the account association succeeded.
+
[NOTE]
====
It can take between 2 and 48 hours for the support entitlement to register and appear in your Red Hat Customer Portal account.
