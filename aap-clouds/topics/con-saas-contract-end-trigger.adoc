= What triggers contract end

[role="_abstract"]
Identify the events that trigger an AWS contract-ended notice and understand how AWS notifies Red Hat to stop billing immediately.

A contract-ended notice is triggered when:

* A Private Offer expires on its scheduled end date
* An AWS subscription is manually canceled
* Pay-As-You-Go metering stops

When this happens, AWS notifies Red Hat that the business relationship has ended and billing stops immediately.
