= Changing your maintenance window

[role="_abstract"]
Align your service update schedule with organizational availability by requesting a shift to an alternate regional timeframe.

Managed Ansible Automation Platform deployments undergo regular weekly maintenance and infrastructure upgrades to ensure system stability and security. 
By default, your instance is assigned a 2-hour maintenance window based on the geographical region of your deployment.

Available maintenance windows:

North America (NA):: Tuesdays at 2:00 AM ET (2 hours for AAP and infrastructure upgrades)

EMEA:: Tuesdays at 2:00 AM UTC (2 hours for AAP and infrastructure upgrades)

APAC:: Tuesdays at 2:00 AM JST (2 hours for AAP and infrastructure upgrades)

If your default regional maintenance window conflicts with your operational needs, you can request to change your scheduled window. 

[Important]
==== 
You cannot request a custom maintenance time. You may only select from one of the three standard regional maintenance windows listed above (NA, EMEA, or APAC).
====

To request a change to your maintenance window, you must submit a support ticket to the Red Hat Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) team.

. Log in to the Red Hat Customer Portal and open a new support case.
. Fill out the support ticket using the following template:
+
----
Subject: Change Maintenance Window 
Severity: 4
Body: 
Hello SRE Team,
I am requesting a change to my maintenance window.
--- TARGET ENVIRONMENT ---
Company Name: [INSERT COMPANY NAME]
Instance URL: [INSERT INSTANCE URL]
Preferred Window: [Select one: NA (2 AM ET) / EMEA (2 AM UTC) / APAC (2 AM JST)]
----

. Submit the ticket. The Red Hat SRE team will confirm once your maintenance window has been successfully updated.
