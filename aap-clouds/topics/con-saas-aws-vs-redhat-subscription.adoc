= Understanding AWS subscriptions vs. Red Hat subscriptions

The term "AWS subscription" refers to your purchase agreement with AWS for
{SaaSonAWS} through AWS Marketplace. This is distinct from a Red Hat Subscription, which governs product entitlements and licensing within the Red Hat ecosystem.

Do not confuse these two separate agreements:

[cols="1,1"]
|====
| AWS Subscription | Red Hat Subscription
| Purchase agreement with AWS for {SaaSonAWS} through AWS Marketplace (Private Offer or Pay-As-You-Go) | Entitlement and licensing agreement for Red Hat products
| Controls your access to and billing for the managed service | Controls your product feature access and support entitlements
| Managed at the AWS account level | Managed at the Red Hat customer account level
|====
